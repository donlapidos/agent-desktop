Trusted apps

Never act in

Agents won't click, type or move windows in these apps at all, even if you'd approve. Password managers are listed to start with.

Routines

Tasks Buddy starts on a schedule, each in its own window tucked into the island. They follow your Agents rules, so anything that needs your OK waits for you.

Routines run while Buddy is running. One missed by less than six hours (the PC was asleep) runs when Buddy next can; older ones are skipped.

Activity

What Buddy changed on your PC this past week: files it made or changed, documents, commands it ran and pages it read. A file can be put back with Undo: one Buddy made goes to the Recycle Bin, one it changed gets its old version back.

About & updates

Desktop Buddy is open source. You bring your own Anthropic API key; everything else runs on this PC.

Check for updates automatically

AI & privacy

Buddy thinks with the AI you choose: a provider on your own API key, or a model running on this PC. It can also work inside Claude's own apps on your Claude plan. Questions, and what's on screen when you ask, go to the AI you choose; with a model on this PC, they stay here. Besides that, Buddy only downloads its voice models once and checks GitHub for updates.
